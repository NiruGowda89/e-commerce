Quick Help
📦 Where is my order? ›
Go to My Orders in your account to see live status and your delivery OTP. If your order hasn't moved in 5 days, call us directly.
🔄 How do I return a product? ›
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused, unwashed items with original tags. Call or WhatsApp us with your Order ID to initiate a return.
💳 When will I get my refund? ›
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the returned item. Amount is credited to your original payment method.
📏 Wrong size / Wrong item received? ›
We're sorry! Please call or WhatsApp us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and your Order ID. We'll arrange a free exchange.
🕐 What are your working hours? ›
We're available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST. WhatsApp messages are monitored on Sundays too.
